# Binders

Knowledge base for producing accurate, on-voice content. Each fact lives on one page; other pages link to it.

## Sections

- [Brand](Brand/index.md): voice and tone, key messages, brand story, boilerplates, visual identity
- [Product](Product/index.md): overview, features, use cases, pricing, roadmap, vision
- [Company](Company/index.md): mission and vision, founding story, team, why now, tools
- [Market](Market/index.md): ICP, customer segments, competitive landscape, positioning, trends, channels

Pages are distilled from Notes/ by the `binders` skill. Ask the assistant to set up or update your Binders.

Empty pages? Use "Set up your Binders" to get the onboarding questions, answer them, and ask for an update.
