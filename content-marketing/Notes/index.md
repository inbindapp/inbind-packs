# Notes

Raw signal: meeting notes, transcripts, research, decisions, and feedback. Notes are distilled into [Binders](../Binders/index.md) by the `binders` skill.

- Name each note `YYYY-MM-DD Title.md`.
- Start it with frontmatter: `date`, `type` (notes, meeting, reference, decision, or feedback), `binder` (the sections it is relevant to: Brand, Product, Company, Market), and `status` (draft or complete).
- Content briefs and drafts in progress live in `Notes/Content/`, one folder per piece (see the `content-brief` skill). Finished pieces move to `Content/`.
- The onboarding questionnaire is created by "Set up your Binders" and lives in `Notes/Onboarding/`. Answer it to fill the Binders.
